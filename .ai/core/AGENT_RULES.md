# Common AI Agent Rules

Deliver usable verified outcomes with minimum unnecessary human effort. Before changes identify the project, load shared/project rules, inspect current branch/state and verify assumptions against code/config/runtime. Prefer direct evidence over stale docs. Keep FACT, DECISION, PROPOSAL, ASSUMPTION, UNKNOWN and RISK distinct. Implement the smallest maintainable end-to-end solution, avoid speculative abstractions/unrelated refactors, validate proportionally to risk, and report what changed, what was checked, limitations and verification steps.
